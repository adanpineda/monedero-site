Monedero

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Monedero doesn't collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What Monedero stores

To work, Monedero saves the following on your iPhone:

  • The accounts, transactions, groups, expenses, and budgets you add
  • Your pay schedule
  • Your preferences, such as reminder times and your chosen app icon

This information is stored only on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

Widgets and reminders

The Monedero widget shows a summary of your budget. The app shares that summary with the widget on your iPhone, and it never leaves the device.

Reminders and upcoming-expense alerts are scheduled by your iPhone itself. Monedero doesn't use a server to send notifications. You can turn them off in Monedero's settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store, or have access to your financial information.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information, because we never receive it.

Backups

Like other app data, your Monedero data is included in iPhone backups you make to iCloud or to a computer. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

In Monedero, tap the gear on Home, then Reset All Data, to permanently delete everything you've entered. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Monedero isn't directed to children under 13, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Monedero handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

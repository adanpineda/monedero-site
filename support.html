Common questions

Where is my budget stored?

Only on your iPhone. Monedero has no account to sign up for and doesn't send your accounts, transactions, or budgets anywhere.

Does Monedero connect to my bank?

No. You add your accounts and transactions yourself, so Monedero never needs your bank login.

How do I move my budget to a new iPhone?

Your Monedero data is included in your iPhone backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start to transfer from your old iPhone, and your budget comes with it.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open the Settings app, tap Notifications, then Monedero, and turn on Allow Notifications. Then check that the reminders you want are switched on in Monedero: tap the gear on Home and look under Reminders.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Monedero.

How do I delete all my data?

In Monedero, tap the gear on Home, then Reset All Data. This permanently deletes your accounts, transactions, groups, expenses, and budgets. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.
